Preamble

These Bylaws and Operating Governance Framework (hereinafter referred to as the "Bylaws") establish the internal governance, management, ownership, administration, financial accountability, intellectual property, business operations and strategic development framework of Enerlytix Technologies, LLC (hereinafter referred to as the "Company").

Enerlytix Technologies, LLC is established with the objective of advancing intelligent energy technologies by integrating artificial intelligence, computational analytics, engineering innovation and sustainable energy systems.

The Company recognises the increasing importance of intelligent energy infrastructure, renewable-energy integration, energy optimisation, smart grids, predictive analytics and sustainable engineering in addressing contemporary energy challenges.

Through its technology development and research-oriented activities, the Company seeks to translate computational intelligence and engineering research into practical, scalable and commercially relevant energy solutions.

These Bylaws shall guide the Company's internal administration and operations, subject to its executed Operating Agreement, Certificate of Formation, applicable state LLC law and other relevant legal requirements.

Article I

Name, Legal Status and Business Identity

1.1Official Name

The official legal name of the Company shall be:

Enerlytix Technologies, LLC

1.2Legal Status

The Company shall operate as a Limited Liability Company (LLC), organised and maintained in accordance with the laws of the state in which it is registered.

1.3Principal Office

The principal business office shall be established at the address approved by the Members or authorised Manager.

1.4Registered Office and Agent

The Company shall maintain a registered office and registered agent as required by the applicable state law.

1.5Duration

The Company shall continue perpetually unless dissolved in accordance with these Bylaws, its Operating Agreement or applicable law.

1.6Business Identity

The Company may maintain its official website, brand identity, trademarks, digital platforms, commercial documentation and business communication channels.

1.7Limited Liability

No Member shall be personally liable for the debts, obligations or liabilities of the Company solely by reason of being a Member, except as provided by applicable law or through an independently assumed contractual obligation.

Article II

Vision, Mission and Core Objectives

2.1Vision

To become an innovative technology company advancing intelligent, efficient, resilient and sustainable energy systems through artificial intelligence, advanced analytics and engineering innovation.

2.2Mission

To transform complex energy data into actionable intelligence through the development and deployment of AI-powered software, predictive models, intelligent energy-management solutions and sustainable engineering technologies.

2.3Strategic Objectives

The Company shall pursue the following objectives:

  1. Develop AI-powered energy optimisation technologies.
  2. Advance renewable-energy forecasting and predictive analytics.
  3. Develop intelligent energy-management software and platforms.
  4. Design computational solutions for smart grids and connected energy systems.
  5. Develop predictive modelling for energy generation, consumption and demand.
  6. Investigate intelligent battery-management and energy-storage optimisation technologies.
  7. Develop data-driven solutions for energy efficiency and operational improvement.
  8. Integrate IoT, edge computing and intelligent monitoring technologies into energy infrastructure.
  9. Advance computational modelling, digital twins and intelligent engineering systems.
  10. Develop scalable technology products and commercial software solutions.
  11. Establish strategic partnerships with energy companies, utilities, research institutions and technology providers.
  12. Promote sustainable engineering and environmentally responsible innovation.
  13. Support technology transfer, commercialisation and industrial deployment.

Article III

Scope of Business and Commercial Activities

The Company may undertake lawful business and commercial activities in the following areas:

3.1AI-Powered Energy Intelligence

Development of machine-learning models, predictive analytics, computational intelligence and AI-powered decision-support technologies for energy systems.

3.2Renewable-Energy Technologies

Development of computational solutions for solar, wind and other renewable-energy systems, including generation forecasting and performance optimisation.

3.3Smart Grids and Intelligent Energy Infrastructure

Development of intelligent monitoring, predictive control, grid analytics, distributed energy coordination and smart-grid technologies.

3.4Energy Analytics and Forecasting

Development of software and analytical platforms for electricity-demand prediction, energy consumption analysis, performance assessment and operational intelligence.

3.5Sustainable Engineering

Provision of computational and engineering solutions supporting energy efficiency, sustainable infrastructure and environmental responsibility.

3.6Energy Storage and Battery Intelligence

Development of intelligent battery analytics, energy-storage optimisation and predictive maintenance solutions.

3.7Software and Technology Development

Design, development, licensing, maintenance and commercialisation of software, digital platforms, algorithms and technology products.

3.8Research and Development Services

Provision of approved research, computational modelling, engineering development, technical consultancy and innovation services.

3.9Commercial Partnerships

The Company may enter into lawful agreements with universities, industries, utilities, technology companies and other organisations.

3.10Additional Activities

The Company may undertake any other lawful activities consistent with its formation documents and applicable law.

Article IV

Ownership and Membership Structure

4.1Members

The owners of the Company shall be referred to as Members.

The identity, ownership percentages, capital contributions and membership interests of each Member shall be recorded in the Company's official Membership Register and Operating Agreement.

4.2Membership Interests

Each Member's ownership interest shall be determined according to the executed Operating Agreement and applicable state law.

4.4Admission of New Members

New Members may be admitted following approval under the Operating Agreement and execution of the necessary membership and ownership documentation.

4.5Rights of Members

Subject to applicable law and the Operating Agreement, Members shall have the right to:

  1. Participate in Company governance.
  1. Receive financial information and approved business reports.
  1. Participate in distributions according to their economic interests.
  1. Vote on matters reserved for Member approval.
  1. Review Company records as permitted by law.
  1. Participate in strategic decisions according to their governance rights.

4.6Responsibilities of Members

Members shall:

  1. Act in accordance with these Bylaws and the Operating Agreement.
  1. Protect Company assets and confidential information.
  1. Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
  1. Respect intellectual-property rights.
  1. Fulfil agreed financial and contractual obligations.
  1. Avoid unauthorised commitments on behalf of the Company.

4.7Transfer of Membership Interests

No Member may transfer, assign, pledge or otherwise dispose of membership interests except in accordance with the Operating Agreement and applicable law.

4.8Withdrawal and Removal

Member withdrawal, dissociation, removal, death, incapacity and related consequences shall be governed by the Operating Agreement and applicable state law.

Article V

Management and Corporate Governance

5.1Management Structure

The Company shall operate under a management structure selected in its executed Operating Agreement.

The Company may adopt either:

  • Manager-managed governance; or
  • Member-managed governance.

The management structure shall be formally recorded in the Company's formation and governance documents.

5.2Managing Member / Chief Executive Officer

The Members may appoint a Managing Member or Chief Executive Officer (CEO) to lead the Company's strategic and operational activities.

5.3Manager

Where the Company adopts a manager-managed structure, the appointed Manager shall exercise the authority assigned under the Operating Agreement.

5.4Management Responsibilities

The authorised executive management shall:

  1. Develop and implement the Company's strategic plans.
  1. Supervise business operations.
  1. Manage approved commercial and technology-development activities.
  1. Develop client and industry relationships.
  1. Coordinate research and technology partnerships.
  1. Oversee financial and administrative operations.
  1. Ensure regulatory and contractual compliance.
  1. Protect Company assets and intellectual property.
  1. Submit management and financial reports to the Members.

5.5Appointment and Removal

Managers and executive officers shall be appointed, evaluated and removed according to the Operating Agreement and applicable law.

5.6Delegation of Authority

Management may delegate operational responsibilities to authorised employees, technical leads, consultants or project managers, provided such delegation does not exceed the authority granted by the Company.

5.7Fiduciary and Legal Duties

Managers and Members shall observe applicable duties of loyalty, care, good faith and fair dealing to the extent required by the governing law and the Operating Agreement.

Article VI

Executive and Technical Leadership

The Company may establish the following executive positions as its business operations expand:

6.1Chief Executive Officer (CEO)

Responsible for overall strategic leadership, business development, operational direction and institutional representation.

6.2Chief Technology Officer (CTO)

Responsible for technology strategy, software development, AI architecture, engineering systems and technical innovation.

6.3Chief Operating Officer (COO)

Responsible for operational management, project coordination, business processes and service delivery.

6.4Chief Financial Officer (CFO)

Responsible for financial planning, budgeting, financial reporting and accounting oversight, subject to the Company's approved governance arrangements.

6.5Director of Research and Innovation

Responsible for research strategy, technology development, scientific collaborations and innovation programmes.

6.6Technical and Research Teams

The Company may establish technical teams and project units covering:

  • Artificial intelligence and machine learning.
  • Energy analytics and forecasting.
  • Renewable-energy technologies.
  • Smart grids and intelligent infrastructure.
  • Software engineering.
  • Energy storage and battery intelligence.
  • Computational modelling.
  • Sustainable engineering.

6.7Appointment Principles

All executive appointments shall be based on professional competence, relevant experience, organisational needs and approval by the appropriate governing authority.

Article VII

Research, Development and Innovation

7.1Research and Development Policy

The Company shall promote innovation through structured research and technology-development activities.

7.2Research Priorities

The Company's research priorities shall include:

  1. AI-driven energy optimisation.
  1. Renewable-energy forecasting.
  1. Intelligent energy-demand management.
  1. Smart-grid analytics.
  1. Battery-management intelligence.
  1. Predictive maintenance.
  1. Computational energy modelling.
  1. Sustainable engineering technologies.

7.3Product Development

Research outcomes may be developed into commercial products, software platforms, licensed technologies, prototypes and engineering solutions.

7.4Research Quality

The Company shall promote technical accuracy, reproducibility, transparent methodologies and appropriate validation of its products and services.

7.5Innovation Management

Major research and development projects shall have defined objectives, deliverables, responsibilities, resource allocations and evaluation criteria.

7.6Commercial Readiness

Technology products shall undergo appropriate technical assessment, security review, performance evaluation and commercial feasibility analysis before deployment.

Article VIII

Relationship with IESST Lab and ICRI-STE

8.1Research Affiliation

Enerlytix Technologies, LLC may maintain a research and innovation relationship with the Intelligent Energy Systems & Sustainable Technologies (IESST) Lab operating under ICRI-STE.

8.2Institutional Distinction

The Company and IESST Lab shall remain institutionally and legally distinct unless a formal legal arrangement establishes otherwise.

8.3Research Collaboration

The Company may collaborate with IESST Lab on approved research and technology-development initiatives, including:

  1. AI-powered energy optimisation.
  1. Renewable-energy forecasting.
  1. Smart-grid intelligence.
  1. Computational energy modelling.
  1. Intelligent energy-management platforms.
  1. Sustainable engineering technologies.

8.4Research Agreements

Any collaborative research arrangement shall be governed by a written agreement defining:

  • Research responsibilities.
  • Financial contributions.
  • Intellectual-property ownership.
  • Publication and acknowledgement rights.
  • Confidentiality obligations.
  • Technology licensing.
  • Commercialisation rights.
  • Dispute-resolution arrangements.

8.5Use of Institutional Identity

The Company shall not represent itself as an independently owned subsidiary, legal division or authorised commercial representative of ICRI-STE unless such a relationship has been formally established.

8.6Scientific Independence

The Company's commercial activities shall not compromise the academic independence, research integrity or governance responsibilities of IESST Lab.

8.7Shared Resources

Any use of research facilities, software, equipment, personnel or institutional resources shall be authorised and documented through appropriate agreements.

Article IX

Financial Management and Accountability

9.1Financial Administration

The Company shall maintain appropriate financial-management and accounting systems in accordance with applicable laws and recognised accounting practices.

9.2Company Bank Accounts

Company funds shall be maintained in accounts established in the Company's legal name and operated by authorised signatories.

9.3Sources of Revenue

The Company's lawful revenue may include:

  1. Software and technology sales.
  1. Technology licensing.
  1. Engineering and consultancy services.
  1. Research and development contracts.
  1. Industrial partnerships.
  1. Subscription-based digital services.
  1. Approved grants and innovation funding.
  1. Other lawful business activities.

9.4Annual Budget

Management shall prepare an annual operating and development budget for approval according to the Operating Agreement.

9.5Financial Records

The Company shall maintain accurate records of:

  • Revenue and expenditure.
  • Capital contributions.
  • Membership interests.
  • Business contracts.
  • Assets and liabilities.
  • Taxes and statutory obligations.
  • Payroll and operating expenses.
  • Intellectual-property transactions.

9.6Financial Reporting

Management shall provide periodic financial reports to Members as required by the Operating Agreement.

9.7Distributions

Profits and distributions shall be allocated according to the Members' economic interests and the executed Operating Agreement, subject to applicable law and financial obligations.

9.8Tax Compliance

The Company shall fulfil applicable federal, state and local tax obligations and maintain appropriate tax documentation.

9.9Financial Controls

No employee, consultant or Member shall incur unauthorised financial commitments or use Company funds for personal purposes.

Article X

Intellectual Property and Technology Ownership

10.1Intellectual Property Policy

The Company shall protect and manage intellectual property developed, acquired or licensed through its business operations.

10.2Intellectual Property Categories

Company intellectual property may include:

  1. AI and machine-learning algorithms.
  1. Energy forecasting models.
  1. Smart-grid optimisation software.
  1. Computational energy platforms.
  1. Energy-management applications.
  1. Battery intelligence technologies.
  1. Engineering designs and prototypes.
  1. Software source code and databases.
  1. Trademarks, logos and commercial brands.
  1. Technical documentation and proprietary methodologies.

10.3Ownership

Intellectual-property ownership shall be determined according to applicable law, employment and contractor agreements, assignment documents, licensing arrangements and third-party obligations.

10.4Employee and Contractor Contributions

Employees, consultants and contractors shall execute appropriate intellectual-property agreements where required to establish ownership and usage rights.

10.5Pre-existing Intellectual Property

Intellectual property developed independently before joining the Company shall remain subject to its original ownership, unless transferred or licensed through a written agreement.

10.6Research Collaboration IP

Intellectual property arising from collaboration with IESST Lab, ICRI-STE, universities or external researchers shall be governed by specific written agreements.

10.7Commercialisation

The Company may license, sell, commercialise or otherwise exploit its intellectual property through approved commercial arrangements.

10.8Confidentiality

Members, employees and contractors shall protect confidential business information, proprietary algorithms, source code, trade secrets and commercial information.

Article XI

Business Ethics, Compliance and Risk Management

11.1Professional Conduct

The Company shall maintain professional integrity, transparency, accountability and responsible business practices.

11.2Legal Compliance

All operations shall comply with applicable federal, state and local laws and regulations.

11.3Data Protection

The Company shall implement appropriate measures for the lawful collection, processing, storage and protection of personal, commercial and technical data.

11.4Cybersecurity

Software platforms, AI systems and digital infrastructure shall be developed and maintained with appropriate cybersecurity safeguards.

11.5Responsible AI

AI technologies shall be developed and deployed with consideration of reliability, transparency, fairness, security and human oversight.

11.6Environmental Responsibility

The Company shall promote technologies that support energy efficiency, environmental sustainability and responsible resource utilisation.

11.7Conflicts of Interest

Members and managers shall disclose any material personal, financial or commercial interests that could influence Company decisions.

11.8Anti-Fraud and Anti-Corruption

Fraud, bribery, misappropriation, unauthorised payments and deceptive commercial practices are prohibited.

11.9Risk Management

Management shall identify and address significant financial, operational, technological, legal and cybersecurity risks.

Article XII

Employment, Consultancy and Professional Engagement

12.1Personnel

The Company may engage employees, consultants, independent contractors, technical experts and professional advisers.

12.2Recruitment

Recruitment shall be based on relevant qualifications, experience, professional competence and organisational requirements.

12.3Employment Agreements

Employees and contractors shall be engaged under appropriate written agreements specifying their responsibilities, compensation, confidentiality obligations and intellectual-property provisions.

12.4Professional Development

The Company may support training and professional development in AI, energy technologies, engineering, software development and sustainable innovation.

12.5External Researchers

The Company may engage external scientists and technical experts through consultancy, collaboration or research agreements.

Article XIII

Meetings and Decision-making

13.1Members' Meetings

Meetings of Members shall be held as required to consider Company governance, strategy, finances and other reserved matters.

13.2Annual Meeting

An annual meeting should be conducted to review:

  1. Business performance.
  1. Financial statements.
  1. Strategic development.
  1. Research and innovation progress.
  1. Major investments.
  1. Membership and management matters.
  1. Future business priorities.

13.3Special Meetings

Special meetings may be convened by the authorised Manager or Members holding the rights specified in the Operating Agreement.

13.4Notice

Meeting notices shall be provided according to the applicable law and Operating Agreement.

13.5Quorum

A quorum shall be determined by the Operating Agreement and applicable law.

13.6Voting Rights

Voting rights shall be determined by membership interests and any special voting arrangements recorded in the Operating Agreement.

13.7Written Consents

Members may adopt decisions through written or electronic consent where permitted by applicable law.

13.8Meeting Records

Official decisions, resolutions and minutes shall be documented and maintained in the Company's records.

Article XIV

Records, Transparency and Reporting

14.1Company Records

The Company shall maintain:

  • Formation and registration documents.
  • Executed Operating Agreement.
  • Membership and ownership register.
  • Financial and tax records.
  • Business contracts.
  • Intellectual-property records.
  • Management resolutions.
  • Annual reports.
  • Regulatory and compliance documentation.

14.2Access to Records

Members shall have access to Company records in accordance with applicable law and the Operating Agreement.

14.3Annual Business Review

Management shall prepare an annual business review summarising commercial performance, technological development, financial position and strategic priorities.

14.4Confidentiality

Access to confidential information shall be restricted according to legitimate business requirements and applicable legal obligations.

Article XV

Indemnification and Limitation of Liability

15.1Indemnification

To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, the Company may indemnify its Members, Managers, officers and authorised representatives against qualifying claims and expenses arising from good-faith performance of authorised Company duties.

15.2Exclusions

Indemnification shall not extend to conduct involving fraud, intentional misconduct, knowing violation of law or other circumstances where indemnification is prohibited by applicable law.

15.3Insurance

The Company may obtain appropriate business, professional liability, cybersecurity, directors' and officers' or other insurance coverage as determined by management and the Members.

15.4Limitation of Liability

The liability of Members and Managers shall be governed by applicable law and the executed Operating Agreement.

Article XVI

Amendments and Dispute Resolution

16.1Amendments

These Bylaws may be amended by the Members in accordance with the voting requirements and procedures established in the Operating Agreement.

16.2Written Approval

All approved amendments shall be documented in writing and retained in the Company's official records.

16.3Internal Dispute Resolution

Members shall first seek to resolve internal disputes through good-faith discussions and appropriate mediation.

16.4Formal Dispute Resolution

Where informal resolution is unsuccessful, disputes shall be resolved through the procedures and jurisdiction established in the Operating Agreement and applicable law.

16.5Governing Law

The Company shall be governed by the laws of the state in which it is legally organised, subject to applicable federal law.

Article XVII

Dissolution and Winding Up

17.1Dissolution

The Company may be dissolved upon:

  1. Approval by the Members as required by the Operating Agreement.
  1. An event requiring dissolution under applicable law.
  1. A judicial order or other legally recognised ground.

17.2Winding Up

Upon dissolution, the Company shall undertake an orderly winding-up process.

17.3Settlement of Obligations

Company assets shall be applied to satisfy liabilities and obligations in the order required by applicable law.

17.4Distribution of Remaining Assets

Any remaining assets shall be distributed to Members according to their legal and economic rights under the Operating Agreement and applicable law.

17.5Records and Intellectual Property

Company records, intellectual property and other assets shall be transferred, retained or disposed of in accordance with applicable legal requirements and contractual obligations.

Article XVIII

Final Provisions

18.1Supremacy of Applicable Law

These Bylaws shall not override mandatory provisions of applicable LLC law.

18.2Relationship with Operating Agreement

The executed Operating Agreement shall govern the Company's internal affairs and shall prevail over these Bylaws to the extent of any inconsistency, subject to applicable law.

18.3Severability

If any provision is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in effect to the extent permitted by law.

18.4Entire Governance Framework

These Bylaws shall be read together with the Company's Certificate of Formation, Operating Agreement, resolutions and other applicable governance documents.

18.5Effective Date

These Bylaws shall become effective upon approval and execution by the Members, subject to applicable law.

Schedule B

Reserved Matters Requiring Member Approval

The following matters should be reserved for approval by the Members according to the voting thresholds established in the Operating Agreement:

  1. Amendment of the Operating Agreement.
  2. Admission of new Members.
  3. Issuance or transfer of membership interests.
  4. Merger, acquisition or major restructuring.
  5. Sale of substantially all Company assets.
  6. Dissolution or winding up.
  7. Major borrowing or financial commitments beyond approved limits.
  8. Material intellectual-property transfers or exclusive licensing.
  9. Related-party transactions involving material conflicts of interest.
  10. Changes to the Company's principal business activities.
  11. Appointment or removal of key management positions where reserved to Members.
  12. Any other matter designated as a reserved matter in the Operating Agreement.

Approval and Execution

These Bylaws and Operating Governance Framework are submitted for consideration and adoption by the Members of Enerlytix Technologies, LLC.
